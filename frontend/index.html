KrishiMart

Fewer middlemen. Better prices for farmers. Fresher food for you.

KrishiMart connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) directly with consumers and bulk buyers — cutting out the chain of intermediaries that usually eats into farmers' earnings and raises prices for everyone else.

How it helps

Better prices for farmers

Farmers list produce and set their own price — no commission-hungry middlemen in between.

Lower prices for consumers

Buying closer to the source means consumers and bulk buyers pay less for the same produce.

Simple demand forecast

A lightweight forecasting tool shows farmers which crops are in demand, based on past orders.

Smarter delivery routes

Buyers can see which farmers are nearest to them, so pickup and delivery routes stay short.
